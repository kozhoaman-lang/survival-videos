# Why it works (production notes)

- Same world, music and cats as Night 1–3 — returning viewers recognise it, new viewers understand it without words.
- Clear underdog story: the "scaredy cat" becomes the brave one. Viewers stay to see the bully get payback.

- Every setup pays off: Nerd's tip → torch; phone left recording → payback video; snack stolen → snack returned.
- A gag or scare every ~10–15 s; one quiet moment before the climax.
- "It brought a friend" sets up Night 5.
