# My Mom Joined Roblox… and Ruined EVERYTHING 😱 (Roblox Cats)

Channel: Pixel River
Format: long-form, ~6 min, Roblox-style cat animation, comedy
Language: English. Each character has its own AI voice.

## Characters (voices)

| Character | Who | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **PIXEL** | Main hero. Orange cat, ~12 years old, wants to look cool in front of friends | young, energetic |
| **MOM** | Pixel's mom. Fluffy white cat, curlers on her head. Loves her son way too loudly | warm, loud, cheerful |
| **TOXICCAT** | Rival (from earlier episodes). Black cat with a smirk and a phone, always filming | smug, nasal |
| **BEAN** and **MOCHI** | Pixel's friends | Bean: low and slow, Mochi: fast and squeaky |
| **NARRATOR** | Only 3–4 short lines | deep "movie trailer" voice (for comedy) |

Notation: `[ ]` = action / visual, `(SFX)` = sound, `CHAT:` = text that pops up in the in-game chat.

## 0:00–0:20 — COLD OPEN (hook)

[Lava obby, last jump. Pixel is first. Bean and Mochi far behind. Huge golden "WIN" pad ahead.]

**PIXEL:** One more jump... and I'm the first cat EVER to beat Lava Tower!

**MOCHI:** GO GO GO!

[Pixel jumps. Slow motion. A system message pops up in the middle of the screen:]
CHAT: `⚠️ MOM has joined the game`
(SFX: record scratch)

[A default "noob" cat avatar with curlers spawns in the air — right above Pixel. Lands on him. Both fall into the lava.]
(SFX: splash, fail trombone)

**MOM:** Sweetie! I found you!

**PIXEL:** ...MOM?!

[Title card: MY MOM JOINED ROBLOX]

EDIT NOTE: Freeze-frame on Pixel's face in the lava on "MOM?!" — this is also the thumbnail moment.

## 0:20–1:10 — HOW DID SHE GET IN?

[Respawn area. Mom spins around, jumping into walls.]

**MOM:** How do I stop jumping? Pixel, why is my body jumping?

**PIXEL:** *(whispering)* Mom, how are you even here?!

**MOM:** You left your tablet on the kitchen table. I just wanted to see what you do all day! Is this the "Lava Tower"? It's very red. Is it safe?

**PIXEL:** It's a GAME. Please... just leave before anyone—

**BEAN:** *(slowly)* Pixel... is that... your mom?

**MOCHI:** Hi, Pixel's mom!

**MOM:** Hello, friends! Pixel talks about you all the time!

**PIXEL:** No I don't.

**MOM:** He does! He says Mochi is "kinda cool"—

**PIXEL:** MOM!

[Behind a wall, a black cat with a phone. Red "REC" dot.]

**TOXICCAT:** *(quietly)* Oh... this is GOLD.

## 1:10–2:10 — THE CHAT DISASTER

[Mom finds the chat. She types with one paw, very slowly.]

CHAT: `MOM: HELLO EVERYONE THIS IS PIXELS MOM`
CHAT: `MOM: 😀😀😀🐱🐱🌸🌸🌸`
CHAT: `MOM: SWEETIE PIE DID YOU BRUSH YOUR TEETH`

**RANDOM PLAYER:** ...Sweetie Pie? 💀

**PIXEL:** That's... not me. I don't know her.

CHAT: `MOM: PIXEL DONT LIE I CAN SEE YOU`

**TOXICCAT:** *(to camera, recording)* Chat, you're seeing this live. Pixel. River's. Sweetie. Pie.

**NARRATOR:** Pixel had lost games before. But he had never lost... his reputation.

EDIT NOTE: Show the chat messages big in the centre, one per beat. Each one = laugh beat.

## 2:10–3:10 — MOM "HELPS"

[Quick gag montage, ~8 seconds each.]

1. [Mom buys an item. A giant pink bow appears on Pixel's head.]
   **MOM:** I got you a little present! You look adorable!
   **PIXEL:** Mom, I can't take it off. WHY CAN'T I TAKE IT OFF?

2. [Mom drags a sandwich item into Pixel's face.]
   **MOM:** You haven't eaten since breakfast!
   **PIXEL:** It's a GAME sandwich!

3. [Mom walks up to a big tough-looking cat player.]
   **MOM:** Excuse me, does your mother know you're online this late?
   **TOUGH CAT:** ...no ma'am. *(logs out)*

4. [ToxicCat posts the video. A view counter spins: 10 → 1,000 → 100,000.]
   **TOXICCAT:** "Sweetie Pie and His Mommy." Trending. Number one.

**PIXEL:** *(falling to his knees)* My life... is over.

## 3:10–4:00 — THE CHALLENGE

[ToxicCat walks up, everyone gathers around.]

**TOXICCAT:** Okay, Sweetie Pie. Lava Tower race. You against me. If you lose, you wear that bow... FOREVER. And I post the video everywhere.

**PIXEL:** And if I win?

**TOXICCAT:** You won't. You haven't beaten Lava Tower once in your whole life.

[Pixel looks at the tower. Hands shaking.]

**BEAN:** *(slowly)* Bro... he's kinda right.

**MOM:** *(quietly, for the first time not loud)* Sweetie... can I race for you?

**PIXEL:** Mom, you can't even stop jumping.

**MOM:** Trust me. Just this once.

[ToxicCat laughs so hard he falls over.]

**TOXICCAT:** DEAL! Oh, this is going to be the best video of my LIFE.

EDIT NOTE: Short silent beat between Mom's line and Pixel's answer — this is the turn of the story.

## 4:00–5:10 — THE TWIST: MOM IS A PRO

[Starting line. Countdown 3... 2... 1...]
(SFX: start horn)

[ToxicCat sprints. Mom stands still.]

**MOCHI:** She's not moving! Why isn't she moving?!

[Mom cracks her knuckles. Her eyes glow. Music switches to epic.]

**MOM:** Sweetie... before you were born, I held the record on every arcade machine in this city.

[Mom does a perfect speedrun: wall jump, wall jump, triple jump over the lava, a shortcut nobody knew existed. ToxicCat stares with his mouth open, misses a jump and falls into the lava.]
(SFX: fail trombone — the same one from the cold open)

**NARRATOR:** They called her... The Lava Queen.

[Mom lands on the golden WIN pad.]
CHAT: `🏆 MOM is the first player to beat Lava Tower!`

[The whole server explodes in cheering. Bean and Mochi lift Pixel up.]

**PIXEL:** *(shocked)* My mom... is a legend?

[The pink bow flies off Pixel's head and lands on ToxicCat.]
(SFX: magic "pop")

**TOXICCAT:** No. No no no. TAKE IT OFF!

**MOM:** It looks lovely on you, dear.

[Pixel and Mom sit on top of the tower, watching the sunset over the lava.]

**PIXEL:** Mom... why didn't you ever tell me?

**MOM:** You never asked me to play with you.

[Pause. Pixel smiles.]

**PIXEL:** ...Same time tomorrow?

**MOM:** Of course, sweetie. But first...

**MOM:** Homework.

CHAT: `MOM has left the game`

**PIXEL:** *(smiling, shaking his head)* ...worth it.

[Final gag. A new system message pops up:]
CHAT: `⚠️ DAD has joined the game`

**PIXEL:** ...oh no.

[Cut to black.]
**NARRATOR:** Next time... on Roblox Cats.

# Why this structure (production notes)

- Gag every 10–15 seconds; no scene longer than ~60 s.
- A recurring sound (fail trombone) pays off at the end when it plays for ToxicCat.
- Emotional turn at 3:50 (Mom: "Trust me") so the video is not only jokes — viewers stay for the twist.
- The "DAD has joined the game" ending sets up the next episode and keeps ToxicCat as the recurring villain.
- Original story and characters: no copied game names or plots from other channels.
